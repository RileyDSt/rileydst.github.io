About the Game

Riftbound is a 2D platforming roguelite prototype made in Godot by a team of 7 for one of my game development classes. You play as a traveler stuck hopping between worlds tied together by rifts, fighting enemies and collecting upgrades along the way. No two rifts are ever the same.

My Contributions

This was my first time working on an actual game with a team, and I ended up getting my hands on a little bit of everything!

I primarily worked on the gameplay side, building the player movement, camera, run system, and parts of the procedural level generation. I also helped with level design, UI, enemies, and whatever else needed fixing — including throwing together a final boss the night before our final presentation 😂

That final boss was an inside joke from the class, so don't expect to understand it if you get that far :)

About the Game

Loser's Out! is an automated Minecraft gameshow made as a love letter to the niche Minecraft gameshow community I was part of.

The concept is simple: compete in a series of minigames, don't come in last, and be the last player standing! The twist? Almost everything is randomized — the games you play, the order you play them in, and even the obstacles you'll encounter within them!

This was the result of a week-long game jam using Minecraft as a base. Did I mention it was made in Minecraft?

My Contributions

The project started from my original concept, and I did a lot of the design work that turned it into the final game.

I designed and built roughly 30% of the minigames, built the main hub, and helped plan out many of the other games and systems. I also contributed some programming, but my biggest role was figuring out what games we should make and, more importantly, how to make them fun in such a complicated format.

About the Game

Crescendoze is a dark comedy murder mystery cleaning game where you play as a member of a five-person bossa nova band living under one roof. After a wild party celebrating the band's 1st anniversary, you wake up to a trashed house and discover that one of your roommates is dead. Threatened into keeping quiet by an anonymous source, you must continue cleaning before the band's "investors" arrive later that day. But as you tidy up the house, you begin uncovering clues that help you piece together what really happened the night before...

My Contributions

As part of the cohort's design team, I contributed extensively to the game's narrative, helping develop major plot points, character motivations, and the overarching murder mystery. My most notable contribution was designing and writing the game's finale, bringing together clues and story elements into a cohesive resolution. During this process, I also coined the term Crescendoze for an object central to the mystery, which ultimately became the game's title.

Beyond narrative design, I also conceptualized a slingshot-based Roomba cleaning minigame that made it into the final game, and contributed to the design of the game's other puzzles and level layout.

About the Game

Remfall is a 3D two-player co-op adventure where two creatures with broken, colorless wings awaken on a muted and lifeless planet after a fall. Together, they must work to find their way back to the sky by restoring the world's vitality through painting and synchronized movement.

My Contributions

I joined Remfall as a Network / Usability Engineer for the 2026-2027 development cycle. In this role, I work alongside the engineering team to develop and refine the game's multiplayer systems and player-facing features in Unity.
